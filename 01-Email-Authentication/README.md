# Email Authentication

Email authentication is the foundation of email security. It enables organizations to verify that emails originate from authorized senders and have not been tampered with during transit.

Understanding these technologies is essential for Security Analysts, Threat Hunters, Detection Engineers, and Incident Responders investigating phishing, Business Email Compromise (BEC), spoofing, and other email-based attacks.

This section focuses on the authentication mechanisms most commonly encountered during Microsoft Defender for Office 365 investigations and explains both the underlying concepts and their practical use during incident analysis.

---

## Topics Covered

- Sender Policy Framework (SPF)
- DomainKeys Identified Mail (DKIM)
- Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance (DMARC)
- Email Headers
- Authentication Results

---

## What You'll Learn

After completing this section, you should be able to:

- Understand how SPF, DKIM, and DMARC work together.
- Interpret authentication results during email investigations.
- Identify common authentication failures and their causes.
- Analyze email headers with confidence.
- Apply authentication findings during phishing and BEC investigations.
- Recognize common pitfalls and edge cases encountered in production environments.
